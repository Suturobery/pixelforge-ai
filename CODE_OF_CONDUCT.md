# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in **PixelForge AI** (GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and Discord) a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone — whether you are a seasoned pixel artist, an indie game developer, or trying pixel art for the very first time.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to newcomers and experienced contributors alike.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the craft of pixel art and game development.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, spam, or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Publishing others’ private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who engage in harmful behavior.

To report an issue, contact the maintainers privately via the [PixelForge AI Discord](https://discord.gg/2f5MvmAqF) or GitHub.
